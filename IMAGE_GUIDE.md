## Best Sources (free, real screenshots)

- **[Planet Minecraft](https://www.planetminecraft.com/projects/)** — filter by "Environment" or "Interior"
- **r/Minecraft** and **r/Minecraftbuilds** on Reddit — search "terrain", "interior", "biome"
- **[Minecraft Gallery on CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft)** — modpack/map screenshots
- **YouTube thumbnails** from build channels (Grian, BdoubleO, etc.)

---

## 💡 Tips

- **Your own screenshots are best** — they're authentic to your portfolio
- Use **shaders** (BSL, Complementary, etc.) when taking screenshots for best visual impact
- **F2** in-game to screenshot, or use **ReplayMod** for cinematic angles
- Keep aspect ratios consistent for the gallery grid
- JPG or PNG both work — PNG for sharper quality, JPG for smaller file size
