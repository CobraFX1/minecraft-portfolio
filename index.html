About Me

Hi, I'm Jacob

I've spent 2+ years building in Minecraft. Everything from hand-sculpted terrain and detailed interiors to full modded survival servers with 100+ mods. I focus on making things that feel real and play well.

Whether it's shaping a mountain range in WorldPainter, furnishing a tavern block by block, or debugging a Mixin conflict at 2am, that's where I'm happiest.
